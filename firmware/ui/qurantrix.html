A dashboard for Quran radio, prayer times, Islamic holidays, Ramadan schedules, Dua of the Day, and a daily reminder featuring one of the 99 Names of Allah.

On the paper

The five prayers, the Ramadan count, sun times, the next holiday, dua of the day, one of the 99 Names, and the radio. Turn a block off. A phone on the tag plays a chime and strikes through the highlighted prayer.

Turn a block off. The top bar stays.

Weekday, date, time, place, Wi-Fi, and battery. Up moves an item toward the left. The clock follows the time zone. Choosing a place fills the zone in.

Radio

The stations the round button plays. One tap turns the radio on or off. A short press of Up or Down changes the volume. Hold one for about two seconds to change station. If the first stream does not open, the next one plays.

Default station: Qurango Tarateel.
